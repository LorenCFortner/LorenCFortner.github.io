Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

ThreeFingeredRC Uploader ("the App") is a personal desktop tool that uploads the owner's own videos to their own TikTok account.

What data the App uses

  • OAuth access and refresh tokens issued by TikTok, stored only on the owner's local computer.
  • Video files the owner chooses to upload.

What the App does not do

  • It does not collect, sell, or share personal data with third parties.
  • It does not run on a server or store data anywhere other than the owner's computer and TikTok.
  • It does not access other users' data.

Removing access

You can revoke the App's access at any time in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions.
